NATURAL GRAINMATERIAL STUDY · ILLUSTRATIVE

The question we’re asking

Can an overlooked by-product become an object worth keeping?

We want to explore whether coconut shell from temple and other sacred-place offerings can become part of a thoughtfully made medal. That begins with respectful conversations, a clear chain of custody, and practical material tests—not assumptions.
