The thought behind CocoHonor

A medal marks a moment.
Its material can hold a whole journey.

We’re at the beginning of an idea: to explore whether coconut shells from temple and other sacred-place offerings can be respectfully sourced and shaped into medals that people are proud to keep.

That possibility depends on doing the work properly: listening to communities, understanding existing practices, finding skilled makers, and learning what the material can and cannot do. We’ll share the facts as the work develops.

What we want to stand for

Good intentions
need good practice.

These are the questions guiding the concept—not claims that the work is already complete.

01

Respect the source

Understand local customs and build any sourcing relationship with permission, transparency, and care.

02

Let craft matter

Work with makers to test a form that honours the material rather than hiding its natural character.

03

Tell the truth

Make specific, verifiable statements about sourcing and impact only when the evidence is there.
